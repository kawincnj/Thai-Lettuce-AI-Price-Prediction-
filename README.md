# Thai Lettuce AI Price Prediction

Research code for **A Comparative Study for Effectiveness of Different Artificial Intelligence Models for Thai Lettuce Price Prediction**, presented at IEEE ICCI 2025.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCI64209.2025.10987352). **Best Presentation Award, IEEE ICCI 2025.**

## Study and contribution

The study compares AI models for Thai lettuce price prediction. Kawin Chamnanchak served as first and corresponding author, cleaned and profiled the agricultural price data, and developed the XGBoost experiment. The best model reported in the paper achieved RMSE 6.67 and MAE 5.33.

Authors: Kawin Chamnanchak, Nopparuj Rattanasiri, Yossawat Imjai, and Pichamon Muroh.

## Repository guide

- `RAC_Project.ipynb`: research notebook.
- `Fix_model.ipynb`: additional model experiments.

Open the notebooks in a compatible Python/Jupyter environment and inspect their imports, data paths, preprocessing, and evaluation split before execution. The publication is the reference for the experimental protocol; a fresh reproduction and a complete dependency specification have not been verified here.

## Data and evaluation

Confirm the original agricultural-data source, collection period, units, missing-value handling, and redistribution terms before reusing the data. Use a split appropriate for forecasting and compare models on identical data and metrics. The results above are paper-reported values, not a new benchmark run.
